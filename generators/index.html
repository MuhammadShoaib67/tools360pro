What these generators cover

Generators make things you need on the spot: a QR code for a link, form or PDF, a barcode for a product label, a strong password for a new account, a colour palette for a design, a random winner for a giveaway, a professional PDF invoice for a client, a recording of your screen or voice, or a timed focus session — and now also a password strength score, a decoded QR code, username and hashtag ideas, a colour picked from any image, a wheel-spun winner, a dice roll or a coin flip. Most tools produce a file you can download — a PNG or SVG, a PDF, or an audio or video file.

Everything is created on your device. Passwords are generated locally and never sent or stored, recordings are captured in your browser and downloaded straight to your device, and QR codes encode your link directly, so they keep working as long as the link itself works.

Frequently asked questions

Are the passwords I generate stored anywhere?

No. Passwords are generated in your browser and never sent or stored anywhere. For safety, save them in a password manager.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. The QR codes encode your link or text directly, so they work for as long as the link itself works — there is no expiry and no tracking.

What barcode types can I create?

CODE128, EAN-13, UPC and CODE39 — downloadable as PNG or SVG.

Can I make a proper invoice?

Yes. Invoice Generator builds a professional PDF invoice with items, tax and totals, ready to send to a client.

Do the recorders upload my recordings?

No. Screen Recorder and Voice Recorder capture in your browser and download the file to your device — nothing is uploaded.

Are the random numbers really random?

Random Number Generator uses your browser's secure random number source, so the results are suitable for draws, giveaways and sampling.
