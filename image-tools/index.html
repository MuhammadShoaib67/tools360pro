What these image tools cover

Most image jobs are small and urgent: a photo is too big for a form, the wrong shape for a profile picture, or in a format a website will not accept. These tools cover the fixes — compression with a quality slider, resizing by pixels or percentage, cropping with aspect presets, conversion between JPG, PNG and WebP, HEIC conversion for iPhone photos, text watermarks, favicon generation and meme text.

Everything is processed locally in your browser, so your photos are never uploaded to a server. That matters for personal pictures: the original file on your device is never modified either — you download a new copy and keep the original untouched.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Every image tool here runs entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device, and nothing is stored anywhere.

Will compressing an image make it look worse?

A little compression is usually invisible. The quality slider lets you choose the trade-off: 75–85% quality typically shrinks a photo several times over with no visible difference, while very low settings will start to soften fine detail.

What is a HEIC file, and why can't I open it?

HEIC is the photo format iPhones use by default. Windows, many websites and most forms cannot open it, so HEIC to JPG Converter turns those photos into JPG or PNG that work everywhere.

Which format should I convert my image to?

JPG for photos, PNG when you need transparency or sharp text and logos, and WebP when you want the smallest file for a website that supports it.

What does the Favicon Generator create?

It generates the favicon sizes a website needs, including an ICO file, from a single image — ready to add to your site.

Can I use these tools on my phone?

Yes. Every image tool works in a mobile browser — pick a photo from your gallery, adjust it, and download the result.
