What these AI tools cover

These tools use AI models that run locally in your browser: background removal for product photos and profile pictures, upscaling for small or blurry images, OCR for pulling text out of scans and photos, speech-to-text transcription for audio and video, photo enhancement for dull or soft pictures, and object removal for cutting unwanted people or things out of a shot.

On first use, the AI model downloads to your device — for the background remover it is about 40 MB — and is then cached in your browser, so later uses start faster. Wi-Fi is recommended for that first load. After that, your photos and audio are processed on your own device, never sent to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos or audio uploaded to a server?

No. The AI models run entirely in your browser, on your own device. Your files never leave this device, and nothing is stored anywhere.

Why does an AI tool take a while the first time I use it?

On first use, the AI model downloads to your device — for the background remover it is about 40 MB — and is then cached in your browser, so later uses start faster. Wi-Fi is recommended for that first load.

Which tool removes the background from a photo?

Background Remover cuts out people, products and pets and gives you a transparent PNG, ready for designs, listings and profiles.

Can I get text out of a scanned document or a photo?

Yes. Image to Text (OCR) reads text from images and scans in your browser, and AI Audio Transcriber turns spoken audio and video into text with Whisper AI.

What is the difference between AI Photo Enhancer and AI Image Upscaler?

AI Photo Enhancer sharpens a photo and lifts dull colours. AI Image Upscaler makes a small image larger — 2x or 4x — adding detail so it stays sharp at the bigger size.

Do I need an account to use the AI tools?

No. There is no sign-up and no usage limit — open the tool and use it.
