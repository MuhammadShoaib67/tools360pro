Privacy Policy

How Tools360Pro handles your information — in plain language.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

The short version

Tools360Pro's tools run entirely in your browser. Your files, images and text are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to us or to any server. We do not ask you to sign up, and we do not collect personal information about you in order for you to use the tools.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect the files, images, documents or text you use in our tools. They never leave your device.
  • We do not require an account, a name or an email address to use any tool.
  • We do not sell personal information to anyone.

Information stored on your own device

To make the site nicer to use, Tools360Pro stores a few small preferences in your browser's local storage — for example your light/dark theme choice, your favourite tools and your recently used tools. This information stays on your device, is not sent to us, and you can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for tools360pro.com.

Cookies and advertising

Tools360Pro itself does not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. However, this site is supported by advertising provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

  • You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services in Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record standard technical information in server logs when a page is requested (such as IP address, browser type and the page visited). This is used only to operate and secure the site.

If you contact us

If you email us, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use these only to read and reply to your enquiry, and for no other purpose.

Children's privacy

Tools360Pro is a general-audience tools website and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@tools360pro.com or use our Contact page.
