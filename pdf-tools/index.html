What these PDF tools cover

PDFs are the format of forms, assignments, invoices and contracts — and they are awkward to change once made. These tools cover the everyday jobs: putting several PDFs into one, taking one apart again, turning images into a PDF and a PDF back into images or a Word document, fixing page order and orientation, shrinking a file that is too big to email, and adding a signature without a printer.

Everything happens on your device. The tools use JavaScript in your browser, so a personal document — an ID scan, a contract, a medical form — is never sent to a server, stored anywhere, or seen by anyone. There is no account to create and no limit on how often you use them.

Frequently asked questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. Every PDF tool here runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device, and nothing is stored anywhere.

Do I need to install anything or create an account?

No. The tools open in any modern browser, on desktop or phone. There is no sign-up, no download and no limit on how often you use them.

Will merging or compressing change my PDF's quality?

Merging copies pages exactly as they are, so text and images keep their original quality. Compressing shrinks a PDF by re-encoding its images, which can soften photos slightly at the smallest sizes — the quality slider lets you choose the balance.

What is the difference between Images to PDF and JPG to PDF?

Images to PDF turns JPG, PNG and WebP images into one PDF document. JPG to PDF Converter is the JPG-focused version: it lets you reorder pages and choose page size, orientation and margins before building the PDF.

Can I convert a scanned PDF into an editable Word document?

PDF to Word works on text-based PDFs, where the text is already inside the file. A scanned PDF is just pictures of pages, so its text first needs to be extracted with the Image to Text (OCR) tool.

Can I sign a PDF without printing it?

Yes. Sign PDF lets you draw or type a signature and place it on any page of the document, then download the signed PDF — no printer, scanner or upload needed.
