What these converters cover

Conversions come up constantly: a recipe in cups, a price in another currency, a number in binary, or the audio inside a video file. These converters cover the everyday cases — length, weight, temperature, area and speed; currencies with daily rates; binary, decimal, octal and hex; and MP4 to MP3 audio extraction.

All of it happens in your browser. The MP4 to MP3 converter extracts audio from your video file on your own device, so a long video never has to be uploaded to a server to get its audio track.

Frequently asked questions

Are the currency rates up to date?

The Currency Converter uses daily exchange rates, so it is suitable for everyday conversions. For a bank transfer or a large payment, always confirm the rate with your bank first.

What units can I convert?

Length, weight, temperature, area, speed and more — the Unit Converter covers the measurements people convert most often.

How does the MP4 to MP3 converter work?

It extracts the audio track from a video file in your browser and downloads it as an MP3. The video is never uploaded to a server.

What number bases are supported?

Binary, decimal, octal and hexadecimal — the Number Base Converter converts between all four.

Is anything I convert stored anywhere?

No. Conversions happen in your browser; nothing is uploaded or stored.
