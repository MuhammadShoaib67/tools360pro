Why people look for a Smallpdf alternative

The usual story is a small one: a form to merge in the morning, a file to compress before emailing it, and the day's free allowance is already gone. Smallpdf's free plan counts every tool run as a task, and the two tasks cover all of its tools at once — so a normal morning of PDF work can hit the wall before lunch, with the only way forward being to wait until tomorrow or start a Pro trial. For occasional use that is fine; for a working day of documents, it is a taster rather than a tool.

The second reason is privacy. Smallpdf is a cloud service: your file travels to its servers, gets processed there, and comes back. Smallpdf says uploads are deleted an hour after processing, and that is a reasonable policy — but a contract, an ID scan or a medical form never has to leave your device at all when the tool runs in your browser. Tools360Pro's PDF tools do exactly that: the work happens on your machine, so there is no upload to delete and no account holding your history.

Smallpdf's free plan allows 2 tasks a day, shared across all of its tools — so one compress and one merge use up the day, and the count resets after 24 hours. Plans change from time to time, so check smallpdf.com/pricing for the current limit.

Nowhere: every tool runs in your browser, so a file never leaves your device. Smallpdf works the other way — files are uploaded and processed on its servers, and Smallpdf says uploads are deleted an hour after processing.

Is there a paid plan or a trial?
