Why people look for an iLovePDF alternative

The first reason is a cap that bites exactly when the work gets real. iLovePDF's free plan has no daily counter, which is genuinely generous — but its per-task caps are tight where it hurts: a PDF to Word conversion takes one file up to 15 MB, so a scanned 40-page contract, a long report or a photo-heavy document is over the limit before you start. A merge stops at 25 files and 100 MB, and a compress at 2 files. Each cap is reasonable on its own; together they mean the free plan is built for small one-off jobs, and the moment your files get serious, Premium is the way through.

The second reason is privacy. iLovePDF is a cloud service: your file travels to its servers, gets processed there, and comes back. Its deletion policy is a good one — uploads are automatically deleted within two hours — and the company holds ISO/IEC 27001 certification. But a contract, an ID scan or a medical form never has to leave your device at all when the tool runs in your browser. Tools360Pro's PDF tools do exactly that: the work happens on your machine, so there is no upload to delete, no two-hour window, and no account holding your history.

Where iLovePDF is the better choice

An honest comparison cuts both ways, and iLovePDF wins on breadth: more than 30 tools against our 10, including a real PDF text editor, OCR built into conversion, batch processing up to 500 files and 4 GB per task on Premium, e-sign requests, desktop and mobile apps, and AI tools. Its free plan is also genuinely usable for small jobs — no daily counter, no account needed to start. If you edit the wording inside PDFs every week, convert dozens of files at a time, or need OCR on scanned documents, iLovePDF Premium is worth its price — our tools do not do those jobs, and pretending otherwise would waste your time.

The trade is simple. iLovePDF is the bigger workshop, and its free tier is a per-task-capped sample of it. Tools360Pro is the smaller workshop where the everyday jobs — merge, split, compress, sign, convert — are free without a counter, an account, a size cap or an upload. Not on Tools360Pro: every tool runs in your browser, so files never leave your device. On iLovePDF, files are uploaded and processed on its servers — iLovePDF says uploads are automatically deleted within two hours of processing, except signed documents, which are kept longer.
