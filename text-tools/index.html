What these text tools cover

Writing work is full of small formatting jobs: hitting a word count, summarising a long article, fixing text pasted in the wrong case, checking a page is not repeating the same phrase, writing meta tags for a website, or citing a source correctly. These tools handle those jobs in seconds, in the browser, with no account and no copy-paste round trip to a server.

Because everything runs locally, you can paste drafts, assignments and private notes without sending them anywhere. The text you type or paste is processed on your device and never stored.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text sent to a server?

No. Everything is processed in your browser. Your text never leaves your device, and nothing is stored.

What does the Word Counter count?

Words, characters, sentences and reading time — useful for essays, assignments and posts with a word limit.

Which citation styles does the Citation Generator support?

APA 7, MLA 9, Chicago 17th, Harvard (Cite Them Right), IEEE, Vancouver and Turabian, for both reference-list entries and in-text citations.

What is keyword density, and why check it?

Keyword density shows how often words and phrases repeat in a text. Writers use it to spot overused terms, and to check a page stays on topic without stuffing the same phrase in unnaturally.

Can I turn typed text into handwriting?

Yes. Text to Handwriting Converter renders your words as handwritten notebook pages, which you can download as an image.

Do these tools work on my phone?

Yes — paste or type text in any mobile browser and copy the result.
