How to use the Invoice Generator

  1. Enter your business details and your client's details.
  2. Add each item with its quantity and rate — amounts and totals calculate themselves.
  3. Add a discount or tax percentage if you need one, and check the live preview.
  4. Press Download PDF invoice and send the PDF to your client.

Bill like a professional — and get paid faster

Clients pay clear invoices faster. An invoice that shows exactly what was delivered, the agreed rate, the total and the due date — with your payment details in the notes — leaves nothing to query, so it goes straight into the client's payment queue. Freelancers and small businesses lose days every month to back-and-forth over unclear bills; a tidy, itemised PDF removes that friction and makes a one-person business look established.

Two habits make this tool work even better. First, keep your invoice numbers in sequence (INV-001, INV-002…) — it makes your records easy to search when tax time comes. Second, set a real due date rather than leaving payment open-ended; most freelancers use 7–14 days. Your draft is saved in your own browser as you type, so you can close the tab and come back without losing the invoice — and because nothing is uploaded, your prices and client names stay yours.

Frequently asked questions

Is my invoice data uploaded or stored anywhere?

No. Everything is typed, calculated and turned into a PDF inside your browser. A draft is kept only in your own browser's local storage so you do not lose your work if you close the tab — it never leaves your device.

What should a proper invoice include?

Your business name and contact details, the client's name, a unique invoice number, the issue date and due date, an itemised list of what you are charging for, the total amount, and how you want to be paid. This form covers all of them.

Which currencies are supported?

US Dollar, Pakistani Rupee, Euro, British Pound, Indian Rupee and UAE Dirham. The currency symbol appears in the preview and in the downloaded PDF.

How do tax and discount work here?

The discount percentage comes off the subtotal first. Tax is then calculated on the discounted amount. If your client also pays part of the invoice upfront, enter it under Amount paid and the balance due updates automatically.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Upload a PNG or JPG logo and it appears at the top of the preview and in the PDF, next to your business name.

Is the PDF good enough to send to clients?

Yes. The PDF is a clean A4 invoice with your details, the client's details, itemised charges and totals — the same layout as the on-screen preview, ready to email or print.
