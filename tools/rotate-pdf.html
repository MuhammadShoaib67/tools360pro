How to use the Rotate & Organize PDF tool

  1. Drop one PDF file onto the box above, or click it to browse your device — every page appears as a thumbnail.
  2. Use the ↺ and ↻ buttons on a page to rotate it, the ◀ ▶ buttons (or drag and drop) to reorder it, and ✕ to delete it.
  3. Check the page counts, and use Reset order & rotations at any time to start over from the original.
  4. Press Download organized PDF to save the result. Your original file is never modified or uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The previews and the final document are built entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your file never leaves your device, and nothing is stored anywhere.

Does rotating a page change its quality?

No. Pages are copied into the new document exactly as they are — only the page rotation, order and selection change. Text, images and vector graphics keep their original quality.

Can I undo a deleted page?

Yes — press Reset order & rotations to restore every original page in its original order with no rotations applied. Deleting pages here never touches your original file.

How do I reorder pages on a touch screen?

Every thumbnail has ◀ and ▶ buttons that move the page one position earlier or later, so reordering works without drag and drop.

My file won't load. Why?

The file may be corrupted or password-protected. Encrypted PDFs need to be unlocked with their password in a PDF editor first — this tool cannot open them.
