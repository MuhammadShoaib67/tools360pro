How to use the AI Photo Enhancer

  1. Drop a JPG, PNG or WebP photo onto the box above, or click to browse.
  2. Choose the output: Sharper, same size keeps the photo's dimensions with better detail; Bigger (2x) also doubles its size.
  3. Press Enhance photo. The first time, the AI model downloads to your device — after that it is cached.
  4. Check the result and press Download enhanced photo.

Sharper photos, without uploading them

Phone photos often come out a little soft, and old photos lose their sparkle — colours fade and fine detail turns mushy. Enhancer websites usually fix that by uploading your picture to their cloud. This one takes the private route: a compact AI model (ESRGAN) runs in your browser and rebuilds detail patch by patch, the same family of model behind the big enhancer sites, followed by a gentle polish — a touch more colour, contrast and sharpness so the photo looks alive without looking artificial.

It is ideal for product photos, profile pictures, family photos and social media posts. Be realistic about very blurry photos though: AI can rebuild detail that is almost there, but it cannot invent a face that the camera never captured. For the best result, enhance the original photo rather than a screenshot or a compressed copy from a chat app, and if the photo is huge, expect the enhancement to take a little longer — everything runs on your own device.

Visitors often continue with AI Image Upscaler, which enlarges small photos 2x or 4x with AI sharpening, or Background Remover, which removes a photo's background with AI in seconds.

Frequently asked questions

What does the enhancer actually do?

It runs your photo through an AI upscaling model that rebuilds fine detail, then applies a gentle colour and sharpness polish. You can keep the photo at 2x size, or keep its original size with the improved detail — your choice.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model runs entirely in your browser, on your own device. Your photo is never uploaded anywhere, which makes the tool safe for personal pictures.

Why does the first enhancement take longer?

On first use the AI library and model (a few MB) download to your device. Your browser caches them afterwards, so the next photos enhance much faster.

Can it fix a very blurry photo?

It improves soft and slightly blurry photos noticeably, but it cannot invent detail that was never captured. A heavily blurred or tiny photo will look cleaner, not magically sharp.

What photo sizes work best?

Photos up to about 2000 × 2000 pixels enhance comfortably. Bigger photos still work but are slower and can run out of memory on phones — if that happens, resize the photo first with our Resize Image tool.

How is this different from the AI Image Upscaler?

The Upscaler is purely about making an image bigger (2x or 4x). The Enhancer focuses on quality — AI detail plus colour and sharpness polish — and can keep your photo at its original size.
