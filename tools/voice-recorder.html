How to use the Voice Recorder

  1. Press Start recording and allow microphone access when the browser asks.
  2. Speak — watch the level bar move to confirm the microphone hears you.
  3. Press Stop, listen back, then download the recording (or a WAV version).

Voice notes, practice takes and quick ideas

The fastest way to capture a thought is still to say it out loud: a melody before it evaporates, a pronunciation drill for a language class, a rehearsal of tomorrow's presentation, an interview answer, a voice note you will type up later. Phones have recorders buried in utility folders and desktops often have none at all — this page is the one that is always one link away, on any device with a browser and a microphone.

Quality follows a simple rule: closer and quieter beats louder and farther. Hold the microphone 20–30 cm away, keep the level bar lively but not pinned at maximum, and record a two-second test before anything important. Because nothing leaves your device, even private material — journal entries, legal notes, unreleased songs — stays exactly where it should.

Frequently asked questions

Is my recording uploaded or stored anywhere?

No. The microphone audio is captured in your browser tab's memory only. It becomes a file solely when you press a Download button; if you close the tab without downloading, the recording is gone.

What format do I get?

The recording downloads in the format your browser records (WebM in Chrome, Edge and Firefox; MP4 in Safari). A WAV download is also offered — WAV is larger but plays absolutely everywhere and suits editing.

Why does the browser ask for microphone permission?

Every website must ask before using your microphone — that prompt is your protection, and this tool cannot hear anything until you allow it. You can revoke the permission any time in your browser's site settings.

How long can I record?

There is no built-in limit, but the audio is held in the tab's memory. Sessions of a few minutes to about an hour are comfortable on most devices; for longer material, record in parts and download each one.
