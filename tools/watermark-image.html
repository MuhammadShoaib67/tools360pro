How to use the Watermark Image tool

  1. Drop an image onto the box above, or click to browse for one.
  2. Type your watermark text and pick a position — the preview updates live as you change any control.
  3. Adjust the opacity, size and colour, or switch on tiling to repeat the watermark diagonally across the image.
  4. Download the result as a PNG at the image's full original resolution.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The watermark is drawn on a canvas in your browser and the download is generated locally. Your image never leaves your device.

Does the download keep the original resolution?

Yes. The preview canvas is the full-size original, so the downloaded PNG has exactly the same pixel dimensions as the image you loaded.

Why is the download a PNG?

PNG is lossless, so the watermarked image keeps the original's quality with no extra compression artefacts. If you need a smaller JPEG afterwards, run the result through the Compress Image tool.

What does the tile option do?

Instead of one watermark in a corner, the text is repeated in a grid across the whole image, rotated −30°. That makes the mark much harder to crop out, which suits preview images and proofs.

How do I make the watermark readable on any photo?

The text is drawn bold with a subtle dark shadow, so it stays legible on light and dark areas alike. Lower opacity (around 40–60%) is usually enough to claim the image without dominating it.
