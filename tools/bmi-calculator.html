Frequently asked questions

How is BMI calculated?

BMI = weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared (kg/m²). In imperial units the equivalent formula is BMI = 703 × pounds ÷ inches². Both give the same result for the same person.

Are my height and weight uploaded anywhere?

No. This calculator runs entirely in your browser — your inputs are never uploaded, logged or stored. Everything is calculated locally on your device.

What are the standard BMI categories?

For adults: Underweight is below 18.5, Healthy is 18.5 to 24.9, Overweight is 25 to 29.9, and Obese is 30 or above. These are the widely used WHO ranges.

Is BMI accurate for athletes or muscular people?

BMI can overestimate body fat in people with a lot of muscle, because muscle weighs more than fat for the same volume. It is a useful general screening tool, not a complete measure of health.

Does BMI apply to children?

Not directly. For children and teenagers, BMI is interpreted using age- and sex-specific percentile charts. This calculator uses the adult categories.
