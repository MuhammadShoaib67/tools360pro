How to use the AI Object Remover

  1. Drop a JPG, PNG or WebP photo onto the box above, or click to browse.
  2. Paint over the person, object or text you want to remove. Adjust the brush size for small or large areas.
  3. Press Remove painted area. The first time, the AI model (about 28 MB) downloads to your device — after that it is cached.
  4. Check the cleaned photo and press Download. Not perfect? Use Remove more and paint over the leftover area again.

Photo cleanup, without uploading your photo

Every photo has one: the stranger who walked into the frame, the electricity pole behind a family picture, the ugly date stamp in the corner. Object removers are among the most loved AI photo tools — and almost all of them ask you to upload your personal photo to a company server first. This one runs the AI in your browser instead: a compact inpainting model called MI-GAN, shipped with this website, studies the area around your brush strokes and rebuilds what should be behind the object.

The repair is blended into your original photo, so everything you did not paint keeps its original resolution and detail. It shines on small and medium objects against simple backgrounds — sky, walls, grass, sand, plain floors. Very large objects over busy, detailed backgrounds are the hardest job in photo editing for any AI, free or paid; if the first pass is imperfect, painting over the rough patch and running it again usually gets you the rest of the way.

Also worth knowing: AI Photo Enhancer sharpens and polishes a dull or blurry photo, while Background Remover removes a photo's background with AI in seconds.

Frequently asked questions

How does the object remover work?

You paint over the person, object or text you want gone. An AI inpainting model (MI-GAN) studies the surrounding area and rebuilds what should be behind it, then blends the repaired patch into your original photo at full resolution.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model is served with this website and runs entirely in your browser, on your own device. Your photo is never uploaded anywhere.

Why does the first removal take longer?

On first use the AI model (about 28 MB) and its engine download to your device. Your browser caches them afterwards, so the next removals start much faster.

What kinds of objects can it remove?

It works best on small to medium objects against fairly simple backgrounds — a stranger at the edge of a photo, a bin, a power line, a date stamp or text. Large objects against busy, detailed backgrounds are harder for any AI and results vary.

Does the photo lose quality?

Only the painted area is rebuilt by AI. Everything else stays exactly as it was, at the photo's original resolution.

Any tips for a clean result?

Paint a little beyond the object's edges, not just the object itself — the AI needs a margin to blend. If the first result is not clean, undo is not needed: just paint over the imperfect area again and run it once more.
