How to use the Background Remover

  1. Drop a photo onto the box above, or click to browse.
  2. Press Remove background. The first time, the AI model downloads to your device (about 40 MB) — after that it is cached.
  3. Your subject appears on a transparent background. Pick a colour if you want a new background instead.
  4. Press Download PNG and save the result.

Clean product photos and portraits — without uploading your pictures

Marketplaces like eBay, Etsy and Amazon reward listings whose main photo shows the product on a clean, plain background — it looks professional and the product stands out in search results. The same trick helps portraits for ID cards and CVs, logos and stickers, and social-media posts where you want the subject on a brand colour. Until now, most tools made you upload your photo to a company's server and often charged for full-size downloads.

This remover works differently: a compact AI model is downloaded to your device once, and every photo is processed right there in your browser. Nothing you open is ever sent anywhere, so private family photos and unreleased product pictures stay private. For the cleanest edges, use a photo where the subject is in focus and clearly separated from what is behind it.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model runs entirely in your browser, on your own device. Your photo is never uploaded, stored or sent anywhere — only the AI model itself is downloaded, the first time you use the tool.

Why does the first removal take longer?

The first time, your browser downloads the AI model (about 40 MB). After that it is cached on your device, so later removals start much faster. The removal itself usually takes a few seconds, depending on your device.

What kind of photos work best?

Photos with one clear subject — a person, a product, a pet or a car — work best, especially when the subject stands out from the background. Very busy scenes, or hair against a similar-coloured background, can leave softer edges.

What do I get when I download?

A PNG at the same size as your original photo. With the transparent background selected, the PNG has a real transparent background. If you pick a colour, the subject is placed on that colour instead.

Can I use the result for my online shop?

Yes. Product photos on a clean white or transparent background are exactly what marketplaces like eBay, Etsy and Amazon ask for. The tool is free for personal and commercial use.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in a modern mobile browser. The first model download is about 40 MB, so Wi-Fi is recommended the first time. Older phones may take longer to process large photos.
