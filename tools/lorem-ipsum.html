  3. Click Generate — new random text is created every time.
  4. Copy the placeholder text and paste it into your design or document.

When should you use placeholder text?

Designers use lorem ipsum to see how a page, poster or app screen will look before the real words are written. Because the text has no meaning, clients and reviewers judge the layout, spacing and typography instead of getting distracted by reading the content. It is the standard filler text for website mock-ups, brochure drafts and presentation templates.

One common mistake is publishing a page or sending a design to print with the placeholder still in it, so always replace it before launch. For a rough draft, fully random text is fine. For a client presentation, keeping the classic Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet opening makes it instantly clear that the words are temporary.

Frequently asked questions

What is lorem ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is placeholder text used in design and publishing to preview layouts without finished copy. It is based on scrambled Latin text, so readers focus on the layout instead of the meaning.

How is the text generated?

Sentences of 5–14 words are built at random from the classic lorem ipsum vocabulary. Paragraphs contain 3–6 sentences. In Words mode you get exactly the number of words you asked for.

Can I use this text in my projects?

Yes — it is standard dummy text with no meaning, commonly used in mock-ups, templates and drafts before real content is ready.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The text is generated entirely in your browser with JavaScript.

Why not just use real text while designing?

Real text pulls attention to the message instead of the design, and its length often changes later. Neutral placeholder text keeps the layout honest and the review focused on how the page looks.

How much text can I generate at once?

You can generate up to 100 paragraphs, sentences or words per click. Click Generate again whenever you need a fresh, different block of text.
