How to use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above — counts update instantly as you write.
  2. Check the live stats for words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, reading time and speaking time.
  3. Use Copy text to copy your text to the clipboard, or Clear to start over.
  4. Use Paste from clipboard to quickly load text you have copied from elsewhere.

Why word counts matter

Essays, assignments and job applications often come with strict word limits, and going far over or under can cost marks or look careless. Writers and bloggers use a live counter to keep articles at the length their readers expect, and speakers check the speaking time before a presentation so they do not run past their slot.

The counts update live as you type, so there is nothing to submit or refresh. Paste a draft from anywhere — a document, an email or a web page — and the totals appear instantly. Because the counting happens on your own device, even a private draft, a contract or an unpublished chapter stays completely private.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded?

No. This word counter runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never sent to a server, stored, or shared — it stays on your device.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute, which is an average adult reading speed. Speaking time uses 130 words per minute, a typical pace for presentations and narration.

How are sentences and paragraphs counted?

Sentences are counted by splitting on full stops, exclamation marks and question marks. Paragraphs are counted by splitting on blank lines, so two line breaks start a new paragraph.

Does the character count include spaces?

Both counts are shown. “Characters” includes every character including spaces, while “Characters (no spaces)” removes all whitespace so you can meet strict limits.

What counts as one word?

Any group of letters or numbers separated by spaces counts as one word. A hyphenated word such as well-known counts as one, and numbers count as words too.

Can I check the length of a whole document?

Yes. Open the document, copy all of its text and paste it here. The full counts appear instantly — there is no file upload and no page limit for normal documents.
