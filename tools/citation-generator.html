  3. Copy the formatted reference and the in-text citation.
  4. For a full bibliography, press Add to reference list for each source — the list stays alphabetically sorted.

Why citing properly matters

Every university and journal insists on a citation style because it makes your work checkable: a reader can find the exact source you used, and you get credit for building on real research instead of hiding it. APA is the standard in psychology, education and the social sciences; MLA rules in literature and the humanities. The fiddly part is never the idea — it is the punctuation: where the year goes, which title is italic, and when "et al." appears. That is exactly what this tool does for you.

One honest warning: generators handle standard sources well, but graded work deserves a final human check. Compare your reference list against your institution's own guide, especially for unusual items like edited chapters, reports or sources with twenty authors. And never cite a source you have not actually read — a perfect format cannot rescue a reference that does not support your argument.

Frequently asked questions

Which citation styles are supported?

APA (7th edition) and MLA (9th edition), for the four most common source types: websites, books, journal articles and online videos. In-text citations are generated too.

Is anything uploaded or stored?

No. The citations are formatted by JavaScript in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded, stored or tracked.

How do I cite a source with no author?

Leave the author fields empty. APA and MLA both move the title to the front of the citation in that case, and this tool does that automatically.

What if there is no date?

Leave the date empty. APA shows (n.d.) for no date, and MLA simply leaves the date out — the tool follows each style's rule.

Should I double-check the result?

Yes — always compare against your institution's guide before submitting graded work. This tool covers standard sources; unusual cases (edited books, government reports, sources with many authors) have extra rules.

Can I build a whole reference list?

Yes. Press Add to list for each source and the tool keeps them sorted alphabetically, as both APA and MLA require. Then copy the whole list at once, with formatting.
