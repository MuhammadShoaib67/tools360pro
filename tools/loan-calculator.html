How to use the Loan / EMI Calculator

  1. Enter the amount you plan to borrow and the annual interest rate.
  2. Set the term in years, adding any extra months in the second term field.
  3. Read the monthly payment, total interest and total payment in the tiles — they update live as you type.
  4. Check the yearly summary for the big picture, or open the monthly breakdown to see every single payment.

Frequently asked questions

How is the monthly payment (EMI) calculated?

With the standard amortisation formula: EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 1200) and n is the number of monthly payments. If the rate is 0%, the payment is simply P ÷ n.

Does this calculator include fees, taxes or insurance?

No. It covers principal and interest only, assuming a fixed rate and equal monthly payments. Lender fees, processing charges, taxes and insurance would be on top of these figures.

Why does the interest share fall over time?

Each payment first covers interest on the remaining balance, and the rest reduces the principal. As the balance falls, the interest part shrinks and more of each payment goes towards the principal.

Can I model a loan with a partial year?

Yes. Add the extra months in the “extra months” field — for example 2 years and 6 months gives a 30-month term.

Are my figures uploaded or saved?

No. All calculations run locally in your browser and nothing is sent or stored.
