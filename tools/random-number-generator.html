How to use the Random Number Generator

  1. Set the smallest and largest numbers allowed.
  2. Choose how many numbers you need, and whether decimals or no-duplicates apply.
  3. Press Generate — copy the results with one click.

Fair draws, honest randomness

Random numbers run more of daily life than we notice: the order students get called on, the winner of an Instagram giveaway, the sample a researcher pulls from a list, the dice a board game lost. Doing it by hand invites bias — people unconsciously favour the middle of a range and avoid repeats that are actually normal. This generator uses the cryptographic random source built into your browser, the same one websites trust for security codes, so every value in your range is equally likely, every time.

Two options cover the common special cases. "No duplicates" turns the draw into a lottery-style pick, where each number can appear only once — ideal for raffles and random orders. Decimal places switch the tool from whole numbers to measurements and simulations. Need names instead of numbers? Our List Randomizer shuffles a list of anything.

Visitors often continue with List Randomizer, which shuffles a list into a random order, or Password Generator, which creates strong random passwords with custom rules.

Frequently asked questions

Are these numbers truly random?

They come from your browser's cryptographic random source (the same system used for security tokens), not from a predictable formula. Every draw is independent and unbiased across the whole range.

Can I generate numbers with no repeats?

Yes — tick "No duplicates" and each number in the batch will be unique, like drawing lottery balls without putting them back. The count cannot exceed the size of the range.

What is this used for?

Giveaways and raffles, picking lottery numbers, classroom cold-calling, sampling and statistics practice, game nights, and splitting people into random groups or order.

Is anything sent to a server?

No. The numbers are generated in your browser tab and never leave your device.
