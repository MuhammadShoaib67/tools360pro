How to use the AI Audio Transcriber

  1. Drop an audio or video file onto the box above, or click to browse.
  2. Pick the spoken language if you know it, or leave Auto detect on.
  3. Press Transcribe. The first time, the AI model (about 40 MB) downloads to your device — after that it is cached.
  4. Read, edit and copy your transcript, or download it as a text file. Timestamps appear below when available.

Private transcription, without the upload

Most transcription websites work the same way: you upload your recording to their server, their computers turn it into text, and your file stays on someone else's machine. That is fine for a public podcast, but not for a private voice note, a client call, a lecture or an interview. This transcriber takes the other route — it downloads OpenAI's Whisper speech recognition model into your browser and does the work on your own device, so the recording itself never travels anywhere.

Use it for meeting notes, interviews, lectures, voice memos, podcast drafts and video subtitles. Because a compact model has to fit in a browser, it rewards clear recordings: one speaker, little background noise and a decent microphone will transcribe far better than a crowded room. Picking the language yourself instead of using auto-detect also helps. Long recordings are handled in 30-second pieces with timestamps, and you can edit the text right in the box before copying or downloading it.

Frequently asked questions

Is my recording uploaded to a server?

No. The Whisper AI model runs entirely in your browser, on your own device. Your audio or video file is never uploaded anywhere, so private recordings stay private.

Why does the first transcription take longer?

On first use the AI model (about 40 MB) downloads to your device. Your browser caches it afterwards, so the next transcriptions start much faster.

Which languages are supported?

Whisper understands around 100 languages, including English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Spanish, French and German. Leave the language on Auto detect, or pick the language yourself if you know it — picking it usually improves accuracy.

How long a recording can I transcribe?

Short clips finish quickly. Longer recordings are transcribed in 30-second pieces and can take several minutes, because everything runs on your own device. Recordings up to about 30 minutes work best; very long files may be slow on phones.

How accurate is it?

This tool uses the compact Whisper model so it can run in a browser. Clear speech in a quiet recording transcribes well; background noise, music and overlapping speakers reduce accuracy. For critical work, always proofread the result.

Which file formats are supported?

Any audio or video format your browser can play — MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, WebM, MP4, MOV and FLAC all work. The sound is extracted from video files automatically.
