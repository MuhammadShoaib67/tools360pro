How to use the Sign PDF tool

  1. Drop the PDF onto the box, or click to choose it — it opens here in your browser, never on a server.
  2. Draw your signature with the mouse or your finger, or switch to Type and have your name drawn in a handwriting style.
  3. Press Use this signature, move to the page it belongs on, and drag the signature into place; the size slider makes it bigger or smaller.
  4. Press Place signature & download — your signed PDF is created on the spot and downloaded.

The print-sign-scan routine survives mostly out of habit. For the everyday documents that actually cross a freelancer's or student's desk — a contract, an NDA, a permission form, an approval — what the other side needs is a document that visibly carries your signature and arrives today, not a photograph of a printout next week. Signing digitally also keeps the file itself crisp: the pages here are never re-rendered or converted to images, so text stays sharp and selectable in the signed copy, exactly as in the original.

Two habits make signed documents go smoothly. First, place the signature where a pen would naturally go — on the signature line or just above your printed name — and keep it a believable size; an enormous signature looks pasted, because it is oversized, not because it is digital. Second, keep the unsigned original as well as the signed copy, so you can always show exactly what changed. When the document also needs pages rearranged or combined first, Merge PDF and Split PDF prepare it before you sign.

Frequently asked questions

Is a signature added this way legally binding?

In many countries a simple electronic signature like this is accepted for everyday agreements — freelance contracts, approvals, school and office forms. Some documents are different: property transfers, wills, court papers and certain government forms may require a certified or qualified digital signature, or a witness. If a document matters legally, check what the receiving side requires first.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is opened, signed and saved entirely in your browser — the file never leaves your device, which is why this tool is safe for personal and business documents alike.

Can I sign on my phone?

Yes. The drawing pad works with touch, so you can sign with a finger or stylus, place the signature on the page and download the signed PDF without a computer.

Can I sign more than one page?

You place one signature per download, on whichever page you choose with the page controls. To sign a second page, open the signed file again and place another signature — the quality is not affected because the original pages are never re-rendered.
