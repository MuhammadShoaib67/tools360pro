How to use the Time Zone Converter

  1. Choose the time zone your starting time is in from the “From” dropdown.
  2. Pick the date and time you want to convert — it is read as wall-clock time in that zone.
  3. Choose the target zone from the “To” dropdown and read the converted time in the result panel.
  4. Use Swap to reverse the zones, or “Use current time” to reset the input to now.

Frequently asked questions

How are daylight saving changes handled?

The converter uses your browser’s built-in time zone database (the same IANA data used by operating systems), so daylight saving time is applied automatically for the exact date you enter. A city can be an hour further ahead or behind in summer than in winter.

What does a UTC offset mean?

A UTC offset tells you how far a zone is from Coordinated Universal Time. UTC+05:00 means the local time is five hours ahead of UTC, while UTC−04:00 means it is four hours behind.

Why can the same city have different offsets on different dates?

Many regions move their clocks forward in spring and back in autumn for daylight saving time. The offset shown here is calculated for the date and time you entered, not just for today.

Is any of my data uploaded?

No. All conversions and the world clock run locally in your browser using the Intl API. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

What is “Local time” in the dropdown?

It is the time zone your device is currently set to, detected automatically by your browser. Its IANA name is used for the conversion, so it stays correct even if you travel.
