How to convert JPG to PDF

  1. Drop your JPG images onto the box above, or click it to browse. This converter accepts JPG and JPEG files.
  2. Reorder images with the arrow buttons — each JPG becomes one page, in list order.
  3. Pick a page size, orientation and margin. “Fit to image” sizes every page exactly to its picture.
  4. Press Create PDF and download the result.

Everyday uses for a JPG to PDF converter

Photos from a phone camera are almost always JPGs — and the moments you need them as a document are constant: scanned ID cards and certificates, signed forms photographed page by page, receipts for an expense claim, homework and notes, or a set of product photos a buyer asked to receive as one file.

A single PDF is easier to email, upload to a form and print than a pile of separate images, and the page order stays exactly as you arranged it. Your JPGs are embedded at their original quality — nothing is re-encoded or uploaded, so names, faces and fine print stay exactly as sharp as the photos you started with.

Need other formats too? Images to PDF accepts PNG, WebP and mixed image sets as well as JPG. Going the other way, PDF to Image turns PDF pages back into JPG or PNG pictures.

Frequently asked questions

Are my JPG images uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is built entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Your JPGs never leave your device, and nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

Can I convert several JPGs into one PDF?

Yes. Add as many JPG images as you like — each one becomes a page in a single PDF, in the order shown in your list. Use the arrow buttons to reorder pages before you build the PDF.

Will my JPGs lose quality in the PDF?

No. JPG images are embedded into the PDF exactly as they are, without re-encoding, so the quality you start with is the quality you get.

Can I use PNG or other image formats here?

This converter is made for JPG and JPEG files. For PNG, WebP or a mix of formats, use the Images to PDF tool instead — it accepts all common image formats.

Which page size and orientation should I choose?

Pick A4 or Letter for documents you plan to print, and Fit to image when every page should match its photo exactly. Orientation on Auto follows each photo; choose Portrait or Landscape to force the same shape for every page.

Is there a limit on how many JPGs I can add?

There is no fixed limit in the tool. Very large batches of high-resolution photos can take longer and produce a big PDF, so for dozens of photos it is quicker to work in smaller batches.
