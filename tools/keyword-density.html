How to use the Keyword Density Checker

  1. Paste your text into the box above — the table updates as you type.
  2. Choose a phrase length of 1, 2 or 3 words to see single words or longer phrases.
  3. Optionally tick “Exclude common words” for 1-word results, so words like “the” and “and” are left out. Density is calculated as: occurrences × words in the phrase ÷ total words × 100.
  4. Use Copy results to copy the table as tab-separated values for a spreadsheet.

Frequently asked questions

How is keyword density calculated?

Density % = occurrences × words in the phrase ÷ total words × 100. For example, a 2-word phrase that appears 5 times in a 500-word text has a density of 5 × 2 ÷ 500 × 100 = 2.00%.

What counts as a word?

Text is lowercased and words are extracted as runs of letters, numbers and apostrophes, so “don't” counts as one word and punctuation is ignored.

Does “Exclude common words” work for phrases?

No. The common-word filter applies to 1-word phrases only, because removing words from inside 2- and 3-word phrases would break the phrases themselves.

Is there an ideal keyword density?

There is no single proven ideal number. This tool simply reports the figures for your text so you can judge repetition for yourself and avoid wording that reads as forced or repetitive.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Counting happens entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Your text is never sent to a server.
