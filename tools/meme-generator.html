The two-line joke format that never dies

The top-and-bottom caption meme is nearly twenty years old and still the internet's default joke shape: setup on top, punchline at the bottom, white letters you can read from across the room. The format survives because it asks nothing of the reader — no context, no sound, no video — just a picture and two lines. Group chats, classroom slides, office presentations and social posts all still run on it.

Good memes are mostly restraint: keep each line under about six words, let the picture do half the work, and write the caption in the voice of the image rather than explaining it. This generator keeps the classic recipe — bold capitals, thick outline, text hugging the edges — and adds no watermark, so the joke you make is entirely yours.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a watermark on the meme?

No. The PNG you download is exactly the picture you made — no watermark, no logo, no credit line.

Which images can I use?

Any JPG, PNG or WebP from your device. Use photos you own or that you have the right to edit; the tool simply adds your text to your image.

Why does the text look different from classic memes?

Classic memes use the Impact typeface. This generator uses the boldest font available on your device, which looks very close; the white fill with a thick black outline is the part that makes it read as a meme.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The image is drawn and captioned entirely in your browser tab and never leaves your device.
