How the hashtags are made

This generator builds hashtags from your topic using templates. It starts with your topic itself as a tag, then adds combinations: your topic plus words like life, tips, daily, community and goals, and a few platform-typical tags for the network you picked. The result is a set of readable, relevant tags you can paste straight into a post.

The suggestions are template-based, not AI-generated, and they are not ranked by live popularity — this tool cannot see how many posts use a tag today. That honesty matters: a hashtag that looks right may be oversaturated, and one that looks odd may be exactly the niche tag your post needs. Treat the list as a starting point, then check the tags that matter on the platform itself.

How many hashtags should you use

The right number depends on the platform. Instagram allows up to 5 hashtags per post or Reel — the cap changed from 30 to 5 in December 2025 — so pick the five tags that best describe the post. TikTok posts usually carry 3 to 5. On X, one or two is the norm, and more than three tends to hurt readability. LinkedIn posts work best with 3 to 5 professional tags.

More is not always better. A wall of barely related tags signals low effort and can make a post look automated. A smaller set of tags that genuinely describe the content — the topic, the format, the audience — reaches the right people more reliably than a wall of generic ones. Pick the count that fits the platform, then delete any tag that does not actually describe your post.

Hashtags that work vs hashtags that do not

The tags that work best are specific. A broad tag like photography is used on hundreds of millions of posts, so a new post disappears from it in seconds. A narrower tag like filmphotography or streetphotography has a smaller but far more interested audience, and a new post stays visible in it much longer. Mix one or two broad tags with several specific ones.

Avoid tags that promise what the post does not deliver — tagging a photo with followforfollow or likeforlike attracts engagement that never converts, and platforms notice. And skip banned or broken tags: some tags are restricted by platforms, and a post that uses one can be hidden from search entirely. When in doubt, search the tag on the platform and look at what actually shows up.

Frequently asked questions

Are these hashtags ranked by popularity?

No. The suggestions are built from templates in your browser and are not ranked by live popularity or post counts. Check any hashtag on the platform itself to see how widely it is used.

How many hashtags should I use on Instagram?

Instagram allows up to 5 hashtags per post or Reel — the limit changed from 30 to 5 in December 2025. Pick the 5 most relevant tags for your post; a small set of specific tags works better than a long list of generic ones.

Can I use the same hashtags on every platform?

You can, but each platform has its own norms — TikTok favours 3 to 5 tags, X usually one or two, LinkedIn 3 to 5 professional ones. This generator adds platform-typical tags for the network you pick.

Do hashtags still matter?

Yes, but less than they used to. Platforms now rank posts mostly on content and behaviour, and hashtags work as a discovery aid rather than a growth hack. Relevant tags help the right people find a post; irrelevant ones do not.

Is my topic uploaded or stored?

No. The generator runs entirely in your browser. Your topic is used to build the tags on this page and is never sent or stored anywhere.

Which platforms does the generator cover?

General, Instagram, TikTok, X and LinkedIn. Pick a platform and the generator adds platform-typical tags for that network — YouTube and Facebook are not covered, so use General for those and adjust the count to the platform's norms.

Should I use the same hashtags on every post?

No — repeat the same set on every post and it starts to look automated. Generate a fresh set for each topic, keep the tags that describe that post, and drop the ones that do not fit. A small set of specific tags beats a copied-and-pasted block.

How many hashtags should I use on TikTok?
