Frequently asked questions

    
Is anything uploaded to a server?
No. The previews and CSS are generated entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere.

    
What is the difference between linear and radial gradients?
A linear gradient blends colours along a straight line at the angle you set. A radial gradient blends colours outward from the centre in a circle, so it has no angle setting.

    
What do the colour stop positions mean?
A stop position is where that colour sits along the gradient, from 0% at the start to 100% at the end. CSS blends smoothly between neighbouring stops.

    
What does an inset box shadow do?
A normal box shadow falls outside the element's box. An inset shadow is drawn inside the box instead, which gives a pressed-in or inner-glow effect.

    
Why does the shadow colour use rgba()?
The opacity slider needs an alpha value, so the hex colour is converted to rgba() — for example, #14141f at 25% opacity becomes rgba(20, 20, 31, 0.25).
