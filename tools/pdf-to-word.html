Honest conversion: your words back, ready to edit

Most people who search "PDF to Word" want one simple thing: the words back in a document they can edit — an old report whose source file is lost, a contract draft, a chapter of an ebook, a form someone exported too early. If the PDF was created from real text (exported from Word, Docs or a publishing tool), this converter rebuilds that text page by page into a genuine .docx: paragraphs in order, larger lines kept as headings, bold kept bold. Open it in Word or Google Docs and carry on writing.

What it deliberately does not promise is the original design. A PDF stores where every letter sits, not what the document means — rebuilding columns, tables and exact spacing from those positions is guesswork that big server-based services spend millions on, and even they get it wrong. Here you get the honest trade: instant, private, free, in your browser — with the formatting left to you, where it takes minutes instead of the hours retyping would cost.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the Word file look different from my PDF?

Because this converter rebuilds the document from the PDF's text. Headings and paragraphs carry over, but columns, exact spacing, tables and page design do not — that kind of pixel-perfect rebuild needs a large server-based service. What you get here is clean, editable text you can restyle in Word yourself.

My PDF is a scan. Will it work?

No — a scanned PDF is a picture of a page and contains no text to extract, so the converter will tell you it found no text. Turn the pages into images with our PDF to Image tool, then read them with our Image to Text (OCR) tool instead.

Are images from the PDF included in the Word file?

No. This converter extracts text only; photos, logos and diagrams in the PDF are not copied into the .docx file.

Which PDFs work best?

PDFs that were exported from Word, Google Docs, a report writer or an ebook tool — anything where you can select and copy the text with your mouse. If you can copy text out of the PDF, this converter can rebuild it.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is read and the Word file is built entirely in your browser tab. Your document never leaves your device, so contracts, statements and personal files stay private.
