What AI upscaling actually does

Ordinary resizing just stretches the pixels a photo already has, which is why a simply enlarged picture looks soft and blocky. An AI upscaler works differently: a neural network trained on millions of photos has learned what edges, textures, hair, fabric and text usually look like, so as it enlarges your image it rebuilds plausible fine detail instead of only stretching what is there. The result is a bigger picture that stays recognisably sharp.

It helps most with small images that need to go somewhere bigger: an old family photo you want to print, a low-resolution product picture for a marketplace listing, a screenshot that must stay readable, or artwork prepared for a larger canvas. Be realistic about the limits, though — the AI can sharpen and reconstruct detail, but it cannot invent information the camera never captured, so an extremely tiny or heavily blurred photo will improve rather than transform. This upscaler runs the well-known ESRGAN model entirely on your device: your photo is never uploaded, and there is no queue, no watermark and no sign-up.

Two tools that pair well with this one: AI Photo Enhancer sharpens and polishes a dull or blurry photo, and Compress Image shrinks JPG, PNG and WebP photos without a visible loss of quality.

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model runs entirely in your browser, on your own device. Your photo is never uploaded anywhere, which is why this tool is safe for private pictures.

Why does the first upscale take longer?

On first use the AI library and model (a few MB) download to your device. After that they are cached by your browser, so the next upscales start much faster.

What is the difference between 2x and 4x?

2x doubles the width and height of your image, so a 500 × 500 photo becomes 1000 × 1000. 4x runs the AI twice and makes it four times larger in each direction. 4x takes noticeably longer, especially on phones.

Can it fix a very blurry photo?

It can sharpen edges and rebuild plausible detail, which makes small or slightly soft photos look much better. It cannot recreate detail that was never captured, so a severely blurred or tiny photo will improve, but not become perfect.

How large an image can I upscale?

There is no fixed limit, but very large photos are processed in patches and can take a while, and low-memory phones may struggle. For the best experience, images up to about 2000 × 2000 pixels work well.

Which formats are supported?

You can open JPG, PNG and WebP images, and download the upscaled result as PNG (best quality) or JPG (smaller file).
