How to use the Convert Image tool

  1. Drop one or more images onto the box above, or click to browse.
  2. Choose the target format — PNG for crisp graphics, JPEG for photos, WebP for the smallest web files.
  3. Press Convert images. Each converted file appears below with its new size.
  4. Download the files one by one. Your originals stay untouched.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion runs on a canvas in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.

Which format should I pick?

PNG is lossless and best for screenshots, logos and transparency. JPEG is best for photographs shared anywhere. WebP gives the smallest files for websites and is supported by all modern browsers.

What happens to transparency when I convert to JPEG?

JPEG has no transparency, so transparent areas are placed on a white background. Convert to PNG or WebP to keep transparency.

Can I convert a whole batch at once?

Yes — add as many images as you like and convert them all with one click. Each result gets its own download button.
