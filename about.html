About Tools360Pro

A free toolbox built on one simple idea: useful tools, zero fuss, total privacy.

What is Tools360Pro?

Tools360Pro is a free online toolbox. It brings together 38 everyday tools in 7 categories — PDF tools, image tools, text tools, converters, calculators, developer tools and generators — so you do not have to jump between different websites for small, everyday tasks.

You can merge, split and compress PDFs; compress, resize, convert and watermark images; count words and clean up text; convert units, currencies, bases and time zones; calculate percentages, age, BMI, loans and tips; format JSON, encode Base64 and decode JWTs; and generate QR codes, passwords, UUIDs and colour palettes.

How it works

Every tool on Tools360Pro runs entirely in your web browser. When you open a PDF or an image in one of our tools, the file is processed on your own device — it is never uploaded to a server. That makes the tools fast, and it means your documents, photos and text stay private.

There is no sign-up, no account and no installation. Just open a tool, do the job and download the result.

Why is it free?

Because the tools run in your browser, they cost very little to operate. Tools360Pro is supported by advertising, which lets us keep every tool free for everyone, with no subscriptions and no locked features.

Who runs Tools360Pro?

Tools360Pro is an independent project, created and maintained by Muhammad Shoaib. It was launched in 2026 with one simple goal: a single, trustworthy place for the small tools people need every day — tools that respect your time and your privacy.
