About Tools360Pro

A free toolbox built on one simple idea: useful tools, zero fuss, total privacy.

What is Tools360Pro?

Tools360Pro is a free online toolbox. It brings together 156 everyday tools in 11 categories — PDF tools, image tools, AI tools, text tools, converters, calculators, developer tools, generators, time & date tools, audio tools and video tools — so you do not have to jump between different websites for small, everyday tasks.

You can merge, split and compress PDFs; compress, resize, convert and watermark images; count words and clean up text; convert units, currencies, bases and time zones; calculate percentages, age, BMI, loans and tips; format JSON, encode Base64 and decode JWTs; and generate QR codes, passwords, UUIDs and colour palettes.

New tools are added the same way the first ones were chosen: by looking at what people actually search for and need every day. If a common task still sends you off to another website, that is a tool we want to build — the aim is one open tab, not ten.

How it works

Every tool on Tools360Pro runs entirely in your web browser. When you open a PDF or an image in one of our tools, the file is processed on your own device — it is never uploaded to a server. That makes the tools fast, and it means your documents, photos and text stay private.

There is no sign-up, no account and no installation. Just open a tool, do the job and download the result.

Why is it free?

Because the tools run in your browser, they cost very little to operate. Tools360Pro is supported by advertising, which lets us keep every tool free for everyone, with no subscriptions and no locked features.

Who runs Tools360Pro?

Tools360Pro is an independent project, created and maintained by Muhammad Shoaib. It was launched in 2026 with one simple goal: a single, trustworthy place for the small tools people need every day — tools that respect your time and your privacy.

Muhammad Shoaib is based in Bahawalpur, Punjab, Pakistan, and works in e-commerce and search optimisation — he also runs Boost360Pro, an e-commerce growth service. Tools360Pro grew out of that everyday work: the small jobs (a PDF to merge, an image to resize, a password to generate) kept sending him to sites that demanded sign-ups, uploads or subscriptions, so he built one place that asks for none of those things.

Every tool on this site is built and tested in the browser before it goes live, and the guides are written to explain each task honestly — including what a tool cannot do. If a tool cannot do something, the page says so rather than promising it. That is the standard the site is held to, and it is why the tools stay free: the aim is a toolbox people can trust, not a page that exists to hold ads.
