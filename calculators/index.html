Frequently asked questions

Are the results accurate?

The calculators use standard formulas — for example, BMI is weight divided by height squared — but they are guides, not professional advice. For medical or financial decisions, check with a qualified professional.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Every calculation happens in your browser, and nothing you enter is uploaded or stored.

What does the Loan Calculator show?

Your monthly payment plus a full amortisation table, so you can see how much of each payment goes to interest and how much to the loan itself.

What is a healthy BMI?

As a general guide, a BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 is classed as a healthy range for most adults. The BMI Calculator shows your number and your healthy weight range — but BMI is a rough screening measure, not a diagnosis.

Can I calculate my exact age?

Yes. The Age Calculator gives your exact age in years, months and days from any birth date.

Do the calculators work on my phone?

Yes — every calculator works in a mobile browser, with no app to install.
